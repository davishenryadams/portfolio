About Me

Engineer, builder, problem solver.

I am a Mechanical Engineering student at Cornell University, graduating May 2027 with a minor in Electrical and Computer Engineering. My focus spans product design, systems integration, and the kind of hands-on building that closes the gap between a drawing and something you can actually hold.

Right now I am conducting undergraduate research at Princeton University, where my work sits at the intersection of structural mechanics and manufacturing methods. It has pushed me to think carefully about how design decisions ripple through into real fabrication constraints.

Last summer I interned at Apple on the hardware engineering team, where I contributed to product development work inside a fast-moving, high-precision environment. That experience sharpened how I approach tolerances, cross-functional collaboration, and shipping things that actually meet the spec.

Outside of work and research I am on Cornell’s Design Build Fly team, where we design, build, and compete with remote-controlled aircraft from the ground up. I am also active in NSBE (National Society of Black Engineers) as part of the Cornell chapter.

I am actively looking for full-time roles in mechanical engineering, hardware, or product development starting after graduation in May 2027. If something lines up, reach out.
